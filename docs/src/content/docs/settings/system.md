The **Connections** group in the Settings rail holds three pages: **Apps**, **Other projects** and **Phone & browser**. They connect Neova to the apps you use, to other Neova projects, and to your other devices.

## Apps

Open it from **Settings › Apps**, or click **Email**, **Telegram** or **GitHub** in the status bar.

Bring the apps you already use into Neova: turn emails and GitHub or Forgejo issues into tasks, chat with your agents from Telegram, and reach them from a Stream Deck or your phone. Click **Add connection** and pick one:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Email account** | Emails in a chosen folder become tasks. |
| **Telegram bot** | Chat with your agents from your phone. |
| **GitHub repo** | Pull issues into a stage, and see pull requests in **Tools › Git**. |
| **Forgejo repo** | The same for a repository on a Forgejo server, such as Codeberg. |
| **Stream Deck** | A key per running agent; press it to open the agent's terminal. |
| **Neova Capsule** | Your phone: voice notes and the Assistant. |

The page lists your connections in sections: **Email**, **Telegram**, **GitHub**, **Forgejo** and **Neova Capsule**.

### Email

Each email account shows its name, its status, its address and server, and which folder syncs to which stage (**Syncing** *folder* → *stage*). An account without a folder and stage shows **Sync not configured**.

The status is **Idle**, **Live**, **Reconnecting…**, **Error** or **Live sync unavailable**. On **Error** and **Live sync unavailable**, the reason shows below the name. Click the status to fix it: it opens the account details, or the sync settings when the mailbox folder is missing.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync now** | Imports new emails right away. Shown once sync is configured. |
| **Configure sync** | Choose the mailbox folder and the target stage. |
| **Edit account** | Change the account details. |
| **Remove** | Removes the account after you confirm. Tasks already imported stay in Neova as regular tasks. |

### How to add an email account

1. Click **Add connection › Email account**. The **Add email account** dialog opens.
2. Fill in **Account name**, **Email address**, **IMAP host**, **Port** and **App password**. Use an app password generated by your email provider. Under **Advanced** you can set a **Username** if it differs from the email address.
3. Click **Test connection**. **Save** becomes available when the test succeeds.
4. Click **Save**. The **Configure sync** dialog opens.
5. Under **Mailbox folder**, Neova looks for a folder named "Neova". If there is none, click **Create folder on server**.
6. Under **Target Stage**, pick the stage. Each new email becomes a task in this stage.
7. Click **Save configuration**. Neova imports the emails in the folder, newest first. Tasks appear in the stage as they import.

### Telegram

A Telegram bot lets you chat with the Neova Assistant from Telegram and get a message when an agent needs you. You connect one bot per project. The bot only works while Neova is running with this project open.

Each bot shows its name, its status and the Telegram user it is paired with. The status is **Connected**, **Not paired**, **Idle** or **Error**. If another app or Neova window uses the same bot token, the status says so.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send test** | Sends a test message to the paired chat. Available while the bot is connected. |
| **Notification settings** | Choose what the bot forwards. |
| **Disconnect** | After you confirm, the bot stops and its token is removed from the Keychain. The bot itself stays on Telegram. |

**Notification settings** has three parts:

| Part | What it forwards |
|---|---|
| **Agent notifications** | Messages agents send with `notify_user`, by level: **Info**, **Success**, **Warning**. |
| **Agent status** | **Agent needs attention**: a message when an agent is waiting on you. |
| **Stages** | A message when a task enters a stage. Pick **All stages** or specific stages. |

### How to connect a Telegram bot

1. Click **Add connection › Telegram bot**.
2. In Telegram, open @BotFather and run `/newbot`. Pick a username that ends in `bot`.
3. In Neova, fill in **Account name** and paste the **Bot token**.
4. Click **Validate token**. When the token works, Neova shows the bot's username. Click **Continue**.
5. In Telegram, send `/start` to your bot. Neova waits for it.
6. Neova shows the Telegram account that sent `/start`. Click **Pair and save**.

Neova renames the bot to match the project.

### GitHub

A GitHub connection pulls issues from one repository into a stage when you ask. Each task links back to its issue. Issues already imported are skipped. Nothing is sent to GitHub. Its token also lets [Tools › Git](/tools/git/#pull-requests) show each branch's pull request.

Each repository shows which issues it pulls (**All open issues** or **Issues labelled** *label*) and its status.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pull issues** | Pulls new issues now. |
| **Edit repo** | Change the settings. |
| **Remove** | Removes the connection after you confirm. The token is removed from the Keychain. Imported tasks stay as regular tasks. |

### How to add a GitHub repo

1. Click **Add connection › GitHub repo**.
2. Fill in **Repository** as `owner/name`, or paste the repository's web address.
3. Paste an **Access token**: a fine-grained token with read-only access to Issues and Pull requests on this repository. **Create one on GitHub** opens the page to make one. The token is stored in your Keychain.
4. Under **Which issues**, choose **All open issues** or **Only open issues with the label** and type the label.
5. Under **Add new issues to Stage**, pick a stage.
6. Click **Test connection** to see how many issues match, or click **Save**. Save checks the connection first.

### Forgejo

A Forgejo connection works like a GitHub one, for a repository on a Forgejo server, such as Codeberg or your own. It lists under **Forgejo** with the same buttons.

### How to add a Forgejo repo

1. Click **Add connection › Forgejo repo**.
2. Fill in **Server**, for example `codeberg.org`.
3. Fill in **Repository** as `owner/name`, or paste the repository's web address.
4. Paste an **Access token** with read access to issues and repositories. Make one on your Forgejo under **Settings › Applications**. The token is stored in your Keychain.
5. Choose **Which issues** and the stage, and click **Save**. Save checks the connection first.

### Stream Deck

The Stream Deck connection shows a key for each running agent, with its status. Press a key to open that agent's terminal.

### How to set up Stream Deck

1. Click **Add connection › Stream Deck**.
2. **Stream Deck app**: Neova checks that the Stream Deck app is installed. It needs version 7.1 or later. If it is missing, click **Download Stream Deck**.
3. **Install the Neova plugin**: click **Install plugin** and confirm the install in the Stream Deck app. Use the same button to update the plugin later.
4. **Place the keys**: in the Stream Deck app, open the **Neova Studio** category and drag **Neova Agent** onto as many keys as you like. The keys fill with the running agents of the project in front, in sidebar order.
5. When the plugin connects, the dialog shows **Connected**. Click **Done**.

### Neova Capsule

Neova Capsule puts Neova on your phone. Record voice notes on the go and talk to the Assistant. Notes go to the Assistant when you send them. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this Mac.

The **Neova Capsule** section lists your paired phones, with when each was paired and last seen. **Remove** unpairs a phone. **Pair another phone** adds one.

### How to pair a phone

1. Click **Add connection › Neova Capsule**, or **Pair a phone**.
2. **Get the app**: Neova Capsule is an Android app for now. Install the APK on your phone. No app? It also works in your phone's browser.
3. **Same Wi-Fi**: put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as this Mac. If Neova only accepts this Mac, click **Allow phones on this network**. macOS may ask once to allow incoming connections.
4. **Scan or type the code**: click **Show pairing code**. In Neova Capsule, tap **Pair** and point the camera at the code, or type it. The code is valid for 10 minutes.
5. When the dialog says the phone is paired, click **Done**.

A notice shows each time a phone pairs, with **Remove** in case you did not expect it.

## Other projects

Open it from **Settings › Other projects**.

Let your agents hand work to the agents of another Neova project, for example another team's. Your agents get access to that project's board, with only the tools its owner allows.

The page has two groups.

### Incoming

The projects whose agents can hand work to yours. Requests waiting for you come first: click **Review** to choose what they may do, or **Decline**. You can also decide them on [Agent tools › Access](/settings/agents/#access).

Below them are the projects you accepted, each with what they may do, when their key was last used, and **Connected now** while one of their agents is connected. **Remove** withdraws their key after you confirm; to connect again, they send a new request.

### Outgoing

The projects your agents can hand work to. Each shows its state:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for approval** | You sent a request. The other project approves it in their **Settings › Agent tools**. **Cancel request** withdraws it. |
| **Approved — finish setup** | They approved. Click **Finish setup** to give your agents access. |
| **Connected** | Your agents can reach the other project. |
| **Declined** | They said no. |
| **Access withdrawn** | They withdrew access. Your agents can no longer reach it. |

Removing a working connection asks you to confirm. Neova removes the entry from `.mcp.json`, and the other project withdraws the key. To connect again, send a new request.

### How to connect to another project

1. Open the other project in its own Neova window on this computer.
2. Click **Connect to a team**. The dialog lists the Neova windows that are open now.
3. Pick the project. Under **Your team's name, as they will see it**, check the name.
4. Click **Send request**.
5. When they approve, the connection shows **Approved — finish setup**. Click **Finish setup**. The dialog shows what they allowed and the entry Neova adds to `.mcp.json`. The key is stored for you and never shown.
6. Restart your agents. They reach the other project under the server name shown.

## Phone & browser

Open it from **Settings › Phone & browser**, or click **Web** in the status bar.

Open Neova from a browser on your phone, tablet or another computer. Your agents keep running on this Mac. This setting applies to every project.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Web access** | Turns the web server on or off. Shows **Running** when it is on. |
| **Address** | Shown while web access is on: the address to open on the other device. Click **Copy** to copy it. |
| **Who can connect** | **Only this Mac** is the safest. **Devices on my network** lets phones and browsers on the same Wi-Fi reach Neova. They see nothing until paired with a code. |

To pair a phone with Neova Capsule, use [Apps › Neova Capsule](#neova-capsule).
